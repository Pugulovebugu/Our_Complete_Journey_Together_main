Bugu ♥ Pugu
A letter I should have written sooner

Our First Girlfriend's Day

A little apology for a page I never made, a little celebration for the girl I love, and a promise for every special day still waiting for us.

💐
Dear Bugu,

A page I should have made back then…

Happy Girlfriend's Day, my love. ❤️

I know this page is coming late. And maybe that's exactly why I needed to make it.

When it was our first Girlfriend's Day, I should have made you a beautiful little corner of our story. Something made especially for you. Something you could open and smile at, knowing that your Pugu had spent time creating something just for his Bugu.

But I didn't.

And I'm genuinely sorry for that.

I can't go back and change that day. I can't go back to that moment and suddenly give you the page you deserved.

But I can do something now.

I can make sure that the missed page doesn't become a missed feeling.

Because even though I didn't create a webpage for our first Girlfriend's Day…
I never want you to feel like you are not worth celebrating.

You are. More than you probably realize.

🌸
If I could go back…

I would make a whole little world for you.

I'd put our memories inside it.

Our silly moments. Our conversations. Our pictures. The little things you said that stayed in my heart. The moments when you made me smile without even trying.

And somewhere in the middle of all of it, I'd write:

“This girl is one of the most beautiful parts of my life.”

And then I'd probably still keep adding things because somehow there are never enough words for you.

I'm sorry I didn't make that page then.

But I hope this page can be my way of finally saying:

I remember.
I care.
And I'm sorry, Bugu. ❤️

♡
My promise to you

Not just on special days.

I don't want to promise you that I will make only the big days special.

I want to promise you something much bigger.

I want to make ordinary days feel special too.

A promise from Pugu
  • I promise I'll keep making little things for you.
  • I promise I'll keep creating little pages, surprises and memories.
  • I promise I'll keep finding new ways to make you smile.
  • I promise I'll celebrate your birthday with all my heart.
  • I promise I'll make our anniversaries feel like our own little festivals.
  • I promise I'll remember Valentine's Day, New Year's, Girlfriend's Day and every other day that matters to us.
  • I promise I'll also make the completely ordinary days feel special.
  • And I promise I'll never let a calendar be the only reason I remind you that you are loved.
✦
The days ahead

Our story is bigger than a calendar.

I don't want our story to be just:

Birthday → celebration
Valentine's → celebration
Anniversary → celebration
Girlfriend's Day → celebration

I want it to be:

Us → celebration.

Every day we get to have each other is something worth celebrating. Every month we complete. Every memory we create. Every problem we overcome. Every laugh. Every late-night conversation. Every “good morning.” Every “good night.”

Every tiny moment that becomes part of our story.

Those are our special days too.

💗
And so…

A missed page doesn't have to mean a missed memory.

Maybe I was late with this page. Maybe our first Girlfriend's Day deserved a page back then. And maybe I can't rewrite that part of our story.

But I can write the next chapters differently.

From now on, I want every important day to have a little piece of us in it.

And if there isn't already a special day…
I'll find a reason to make one.

Because you deserve to be celebrated. Not because it's Girlfriend's Day. Not because it's Valentine's Day. Not because it's your birthday.

But simply because…
you're my Bugu.
And I'm your Pugu.

A promise from your Pugu

Happy Girlfriend's Day,
my Bugu. 💐

I'm sorry I didn't make this for you when I should have. But thank you for giving me another chance to turn a missed moment into a beautiful memory.

This page is not only an apology. It's a promise.

A promise that I will keep making our days special — birthdays, anniversaries, Valentine's Days, New Year's, Girlfriend's Days, and all the random little days in between.

I'll keep creating memories with you. I'll keep choosing you. I'll keep loving you. And I'll keep finding new ways to tell you how important you are to me.

Because our story deserves more than a few special dates on a calendar.
Our whole story is special.

Forever your Pugu ❤️
